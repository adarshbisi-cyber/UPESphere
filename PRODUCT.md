# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Indian university students — no secondary audience. They're juggling grade sheets, attendance thresholds, timetables, and CGPA targets across scattered spreadsheets and one-off calculator sites, usually while stressed about a specific number (will I clear the attendance cutoff, what CGPA do I need next semester). They check this often, not once.

## Product Purpose

UPESphere is a one-stop solution for a student's academic life. Instead of re-entering the same data into a different calculator every time, a student uploads or enters it once and UPESphere remembers it — grade history, timetable, attendance — turning repeated one-off calculations into a running academic record. Success looks like students returning to their dashboard as a habit, the way they'd check a banking app, rather than visiting a calculator site once and leaving.

## Positioning

It's not a one-off calculation — it remembers your real grade history, timetable, and progress, and gives you insight over time instead of a single instant number.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, trustworthy, modern — the register of a well-built fintech or analytics tool, not a playful student toy. Every screen should feel like it's handling something the student cares about getting right.

## Anti-references

Not a cluttered, ad-heavy "free calculator" site. Avoid the generic edtech-slop look: banner ads, gamified badges, cartoon mascots, anything that undercuts the fintech-grade trust the product is going for.

## Design Principles

- **Memory over computation.** Every calculator ties back to the student's saved history; a number that appears once and vanishes is a missed opportunity to build the running record that's the actual product.
- **Consolidation, not another silo.** New features should reduce the number of places a student has to check, not add one more tab to their routine.
- **Correctness reads as confidence.** Grade math and attendance thresholds must never look approximate or hand-wavy — precision is part of the trust the sharp/fintech personality depends on.
- **Quiet over loud.** Restraint over cute, gamified, or ad-driven edtech tropes; the product earns attention instead of shouting for it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA, with reduced-motion support. No further specific requirements at this time.
